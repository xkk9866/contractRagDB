\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\system\ sits at the intersection of semantic-operator query
optimization, adaptive RAG, and distribution-free risk control; we
position it against each line in turn.

\noindent\textbf{Semantic operator systems.} Palimpzest
\cite{liu2025palimpzest}, LOTUS~\cite{patel2024lotus},
DocETL~\cite{shankar2024docetl}, and DSPy~\cite{khattab2023dspy} compile
declarative AI pipelines and search plan spaces of models, prompts, and
operator implementations. Abacus~\cite{russo2025abacus} is the strongest
optimizer in this line: an MAB-style sampler that estimates each physical
plan's quality/cost/latency on validation data and returns the
estimate-feasible cost-optimal plan. All of these optimize \emph{workload
level, single-plan} deployments and compare \emph{point estimates} to
constraints; none provides a finite-sample statement about the deployed
plan's true violation rate, per-query adaptivity, or freshness/citation
semantics. \system\ is complementary in spirit --- our rewrite lattice is a
plan space in their sense --- but replaces estimate-feasibility with
certification and static plans with certified adaptive policies.

\noindent\textbf{Adaptive RAG and LLM routing.}
Adaptive-RAG~\cite{jeong2024adaptiverag} routes by predicted question
complexity; Self-RAG~\cite{asai2024selfrag} learns reflection tokens to
decide retrieval, and active/corrective retrieval refines evidence
during generation~\cite{jiang2023flare,yan2024corrective,xu2024recomp};
FrugalGPT~\cite{chen2023frugalgpt},
RouteLLM~\cite{ong2024routellm}, Hybrid-LLM~\cite{ding2024hybridllm},
and AutoMix~\cite{aggarwal2024automix} cascade or route across model
tiers, with RouterBench~\cite{hu2024routerbench} standardizing their
evaluation. These systems reduce cost effectively but their
selection rules are tuned empirically; the realized violation rate is
whatever it happens to be --- our experiments show 15--50\% contract
violations across calibration draws. \system\ subsumes routing as one
candidate family (U-family) and gives it the missing guarantee. Confident
adaptive computation for encoder--decoders~\cite{schuster2022calm} and
conformal cascades~\cite{fisch2021admission} calibrate early exits for a
\emph{single model's} layers or a cascade with shared scores; \system\
calibrates heterogeneous multi-source pipelines with multi-dimensional
contracts.

\noindent\textbf{Distribution-free risk control for RAG.}
TRAQ~\cite{li2024traq} builds conformal prediction sets over retrieved
passages and answers; C-RAG~\cite{kang2024crag} certifies generation risk
bounds; conformal language modeling and conformal factuality calibrate
stopping and abstention rules on fixed
models~\cite{quach2024conformallm,mohri2024factuality}, with recent
extensions to conditional validity and alignment
filtering~\cite{cherian2024validity,gui2024conformalalign};
semantic-entropy methods detect hallucinations without gold
labels~\cite{farquhar2024semantic}. These works establish that RAG
risk can be controlled distribution-free, but they treat the pipeline as
given: no plan search, no cost objective, no adaptive escalation, no
drift handling. \system\ uses the same statistical machinery ---
Learn-then-Test~\cite{angelopoulos2021ltt} with Hoeffding--Bentkus
p-values, conformal risk control~\cite{angelopoulos2024crc} --- but embeds
it \emph{inside a query optimizer}, where the tested objects are policies
over a plan lattice and the objective is cost.

\noindent\textbf{Query optimization and adaptive execution.} Our framing
descends from cost-based optimization~\cite{selinger1979access,
graefe1995cascades} and mid-query re-optimization
\cite{kabra1998efficient,markl2004robust}. The analogy is precise:
sufficiency scores are runtime cardinality feedback, vouchers are the cost
model, certification is the execution engine's correctness contract, and
the e-process monitor is progressive re-optimization triggered by
statistics rather than row counts. What changes is the semantics of
correctness --- approximate, statistical, and contract-relative rather than
exact --- which is why the classical machinery must be rebuilt on
hypothesis tests instead of algebraic equivalences.

\noindent\textbf{Anytime-valid inference.} E-processes and test
supermartingales~\cite{ville1939etude,shafer2021testing,ramdas2023savi,
grunwald2024safe}
underpin our monitor; we adopt the mixture-restart construction of
e-detectors~\cite{shin2024edetectors} to obtain bounded false-alarm rates
with fast detection under late drift. Our audited violation indicators
build on RAG evaluation frameworks~\cite{es2024ragas,saadfalcon2024ares,
ru2024ragchecker} and LLM-judge protocols~\cite{zheng2023judging}, whose
subsampling cost we account for explicitly
(Sec.~\ref{sec:exp-drift}).
