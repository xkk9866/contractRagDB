\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Relevant work spans retrieval-augmented generation, adaptive LLM
inference, distribution-free risk control, and runtime monitoring.

\subsection{Retrieval-augmented generation and knowledge-enhanced LLMs}
RAG grounds LLM outputs in retrieved evidence to mitigate hallucination
and knowledge staleness \citep{lewis2020rag,gao2024ragsurvey,
yang2025integrating}. Recent methods focus on learnable retrieval
mechanisms. AIR-RAG uses adaptive iterative feedback to improve ranking
and document refinement \citep{han2026airrag}. RAG-LER trains a
confidence-weighted reranker from language-model supervision
\citep{zhai2025ragler}. IRAGKR combines retrieval gating, query
expansion, and fine-grained knowledge compression
\citep{du2025iragkr}. Cue RAG introduces a cue-memory representation
and dynamic cutoff mechanism \citep{fu2025cuerag}, while UA-RAG triggers
retrieval from complementary internal uncertainty signals
\citep{niu2026uarag}. These models improve the frontier from which a
policy can choose. \system turns such frontiers into certifiable
progressive policies by controlling deployment risk across their
candidate actions. Learned retrievers and rerankers can therefore serve
as components of the certified execution family.

Prior work also improves the acquisition process
through hybrid retrieval
over tables and text \citep{chen2020hybridqa}, self-reflective
retrieval decisions \citep{asai2024selfrag}, active and corrective
retrieval during generation
\citep{jiang2023flare,yan2024corrective}, evidence compression
\citep{xu2024recomp}, cost-conscious QA system design under resource
constraints \citep{budur2024openqa}, calibration between parametric
and retrieved knowledge \citep{deng2026collaborative}, and benchmark
suites that
stress heterogeneous and time-sensitive knowledge sources
\citep{yang2024cragbench,stelmakh2022asqa,amouyal2022qampari,
gao2023alce,chen2024rgb}. Automated evaluation frameworks measure RAG
quality after execution
\citep{es2024ragas,saadfalcon2024ares,ru2024ragchecker,tang2024minicheck}, typically via
LLM judges \citep{zheng2023judging}, but do not constrain deployed
behavior. Declarative pipeline frameworks, including Palimpzest
\citep{liu2025palimpzest}, LOTUS \citep{patel2024lotus}, DocETL
\citep{shankar2024docetl}, DSPy \citep{khattab2023dspy}, and the
Abacus optimizer \citep{russo2025abacus}, compile semantic operator
pipelines and search spaces of models, prompts, and operator
implementations against validation estimates. These systems optimize
average quality or cost but compare point estimates to constraints;
none bounds the deployed pipeline's true violation rate with
finite-sample confidence. ALTO \citep{santhanam2024alto} addresses
network orchestration for compound AI pipelines but does not certify
output risk. \system\ keeps learned adaptive computation but separates
policy scoring from distribution-free certification.

\subsection{Adaptive inference: LLM routing and cascades}
Cost-aware inference systems route queries to the cheapest adequate
model or cascade through model tiers: FrugalGPT \citep{chen2023frugalgpt},
RouteLLM \citep{ong2024routellm}, Hybrid-LLM \citep{ding2024hybridllm},
AutoMix \citep{aggarwal2024automix}, and Adaptive-RAG's
complexity-based
routing \citep{jeong2024adaptiverag}, with RouterBench
\citep{hu2024routerbench} standardizing their evaluation. Confident
adaptive computation
\citep{schuster2022calm} and conformal cascades \citep{fisch2021admission}
calibrate early exits for a single model or a cascade with shared
scores. These methods reduce cost effectively, but their selection
rules are tuned empirically, so the realized violation rate is whatever
it happens to be --- our experiments measure 16--64\% contract
violations across calibration draws for representative members of this
family. Unlike prior routing work, \system\ subsumes routing as one
candidate family within a certified policy space and supplies the
missing feasibility guarantee; unlike single-model early exits, it
calibrates heterogeneous multi-source pipelines under
multi-dimensional contracts.

\subsection{Distribution-free risk control for language models}
Conformal prediction and risk control give distribution-free,
finite-sample guarantees
\citep{vovk2005algorithmic,angelopoulos2024crc}.
Learn-then-Test \citep{angelopoulos2021ltt} casts hyperparameter
selection as multiple testing with Hoeffding--Bentkus p-values
\citep{bentkus2004hoeffding}; TRAQ \citep{li2024traq} builds conformal
sets over retrieved passages and answers; C-RAG \citep{kang2024crag}
certifies generation risk for a given pipeline; conformal language
modeling and conformal factuality calibrate stopping and abstention
rules on fixed models
\citep{quach2024conformallm,mohri2024factuality}, with recent
extensions to conditional validity and alignment filtering
\citep{cherian2024validity,gui2024conformalalign}; semantic-entropy
methods detect hallucinations without gold labels
\citep{farquhar2024semantic}. A complementary line stress-tests LLM
reliability offline, e.g., by logic-grounded metamorphic testing
\citep{zhou2026lgmt}; such audits expose defects but issue no
quantitative guarantee about a deployed configuration. These results
show that RAG risk can be controlled without distributional assumptions,
but they
treat the pipeline as given: no plan search, no cost objective, no
adaptive escalation, and no drift handling. \system\ applies these tests
inside an optimizer. The tested objects are adaptive policies over a
rewrite lattice, and the objective is monetary cost. The fixed-sequence
procedure preserves validity as the candidate space grows.

\subsection{Runtime monitoring and drift adaptation in knowledge systems}
Deployed knowledge systems drift: workload mix shifts, indexes fall
behind the world, and external APIs degrade. Anytime-valid inference
--- test supermartingales and e-processes
\citep{ville1939etude,shafer2021testing,ramdas2023savi,
waudbysmith2024betting,grunwald2024safe}
--- provides
sequential tests with always-valid error control, and e-detectors
\citep{shin2024edetectors} extend them to changepoint detection.
Database systems faced an analogous problem in mid-query
re-optimization \citep{kabra1998efficient,markl2004robust,deng2007spc}, re-planning
when runtime observations diverge from optimizer estimates. \system\
applies this approach to knowledge services: a restart-mixture
e-detector with a proven change-time-independent delay bound watches
audited violations, an $O(1)$ recursion localizes the changepoint, and
a certified recalibration loop restores the contract on the post-change
distribution. To our knowledge, no prior RAG or LLM-cascade system
closes this loop with statistical guarantees at every stage.
