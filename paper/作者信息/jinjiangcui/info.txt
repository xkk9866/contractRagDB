Jinjiang Cui is a Researcher and doctoral supervisor at the Suzhou Institute of Biomedical Engineering and Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, where he serves as Director of the Rehabilitation and Therapy Research Division. He is a member of the National Technical Committee on Optics and Photonics Standardization, a member of the Human Factors Engineering Committee of the China Association for Medical Devices Industry, and an expert reviewer for innovative medical devices in China. His research focuses on advanced medical light-source technologies, mechanisms of phototherapy, optical imaging, therapeutic intervention, and broader applications in optical health.
