Kang Yao is a Ph.D. student at the School of Biomedical Engineering (Suzhou), Division of Life Science and Medicine, University of Science and Technology of China, China. His research interests include large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, multi-agent reasoning, and intelligent decision-support systems. He has worked on LLM-based service recommendation, control-flow-guided RAG, and multi-agent collaborative reasoning. He has participated in national and industrial research projects on industrial knowledge bases, compliance intelligence, and knowledge-enhanced decision support.
