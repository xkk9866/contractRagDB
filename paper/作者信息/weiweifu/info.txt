Weiwei Fu is a Researcher Level III and doctoral supervisor at the Suzhou Institute of Biomedical Engineering and Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, where he serves as a member of the Institute Affairs Committee, Chief Engineer, and Director of the Medical Imaging Laboratory. He has served as Chief Scientist of the National Key R&D Program of China and the Basic Strengthening Program, and as Technical Commander-in-Chief of a national major project. He is also a recipient of the CAS stable-support youth team program and an outstanding member of the CAS Youth Innovation Promotion Association. His academic service includes leadership roles in professional committees related to intelligent healthcare, health management, occupational mental health, and myopia prevention. His research focuses on artificial intelligence, instrumentation science, cognitive neuroscience, and psychology, with emphasis on intelligent scientific instruments and embodied intelligent robotics, digital health and performance enhancement, and bio-interdisciplinary research and biosafety.
